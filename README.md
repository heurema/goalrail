### The problem

Coding agents can help a small product team ship more. The surrounding work is easy to underestimate.

Someone still has to give agents the right project context, define workflows and permissions, build checks and repair loops, collect evidence, and keep the setup aligned as the codebase and models change.

Large companies can dedicate an enabling team to this layer. A small team may spend more on building and maintaining it than it saves on product development.

### What we are exploring

Years ago, companies outsourced software development when they could not justify a full internal team. We think a similar service may emerge one layer higher.

Goalrail works with an existing product team and its real repository. We map how changes move from an idea to a reviewed result, collect the relevant context and constraints, and build controlled rails around the coding agents the team already uses.

The product team keeps ownership of priorities, product decisions, and review. We maintain the workflow, verification, permissions, and evidence that make agent-assisted delivery more repeatable.

### Who this is for

We are looking for small product teams that:

- already use coding agents in real development;
- feel bottlenecks in process, quality, review, or repeatability;
- want to improve their existing workflow without building an internal agent-platform team.

### Current status

This is still a working hypothesis. The application is being rebuilt around it. The service can already be tested on one real project with a narrow scope and a clear success condition.
